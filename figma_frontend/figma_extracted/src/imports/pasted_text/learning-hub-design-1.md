Bonus (Highly Recommended)

To make this feel like a world-class enterprise product, add these advanced capabilities:

Personalized homepage based on the employee's role, business unit, and learning history.
Learning campaign banners that admins can schedule and rotate.
Power BI-style executive dashboard with drill-down analytics for leaders.
AI assistant ("Ask Learning Hub") that answers questions like "What leadership programs are available for first-time managers?" or "Show all AI workshops this quarter."
Quick Actions panel with one-click access to Register, View Calendar, Download Certificate, Nominate a Team Member, and Submit Feedback.
Role-based dashboards for Employees, Managers, Leadership, L&D Admins, and Faculty.
Content management system (CMS) so admins can manage banners, announcements, featured programs, and resources without developer support.
Dynamic homepage widgets that automatically surface new programs, deadlines, trending learning topics, and recent achievements.
Microsoft 365 integration (Teams, Outlook, SharePoint, Azure AD) for a seamless employee experience.
Real-time analytics showing registrations, attendance, completions, department participation, and learning adoption.
